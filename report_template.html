    Ideology Fit Inspector

    Everything fit.py run prints, plus three views the terminal cannot give you: what theory claimed against what the corpus supports, where the extractor and the model disagree, and who the model thinks each actor is.

    The fit

    Each stage adds one layer, warm-started from the last. held-out is the Poisson rate likelihood on held-out cells — the predictive number, and the one with no leak to correct for, since v3.2 has no free per-unit parameter.

    Theory against the fit

    C1 and C2 are priors in v3.2, not bounds: C1 shifts the mean of an ideology's profile draw by ±α, C2 shifts the support of its network up to ω. The data is free to disagree with either, and what it does with that freedom is the result. Pick an ideology.

    Extractor against model

    For every actor and node: the rate the extraction implies (codings per unit) against the rate the fitted model predicts. A cell far off the diagonal is either a real surprise about the actor or a bad coding — which is what makes this list the hand-checking queue. Sort by log ratio to put the largest disagreements first, then click any row to read the units behind it.

    one actor × node celldiagonal = model agrees with extractor

    Edge blocks

    C5 admits same-type and adjacent-type pairs and forbids the three rung-skipping ones, so the network has a block structure the theory put there. The matrix is those blocks: pick a layer, then click a block to read its edges. Section 5's warning is what to watch for — intra-rung blocks, B–B above all, compete with cross-rung edges for the same co-occurrence, and cross-rung structure thins without anything announcing it.

    By tweet

    One row per extracted concept, with the extractor's confidence beside the model's expectation. The two are on the same scale — both are the probability that the concept is present — but they are not the same kind of claim: the extractor read this text, while the model knows only who wrote it and when. Every tweet by one actor in one year carries the same expectation, so a large gap is a statement about the actor's rates, not about the model disagreeing with a reading.

    Actors

    Membership is a softmax over the ideologies plus a background coordinate. background is the share of an actor's speech the model calls unideological; entropy is taken over the ideology margin only, because spread across facets within one ideology is specialisation, not straddling. Pinned actors (C4) have their margin fixed and are what hold the labels in place.

    Diagnostics

    The residual statistics, reproduced from the same functions the terminal report prints.

    Structural coherence (C6)

    Ck is edge mass inside the ideology's own support over mass crossing its boundary, against a null of random supports matched on size, node type and degree quartile. Below the null is a reportable failure, not a fitting error: a component whose structure leaks across its own boundary is either mis-supported or not an ideology by this framework's definition.

    Contestation typology

    Logical incoherence (C3)

    Ideologies holding more than one member of an incompatible set — what C3 asserts cannot happen. The mass column is the quantity C3 actually constrains: on rare nodes it sits far under π̄, so the cap as written cannot reach the contradiction.

    Ladder test (C5)

    R above 1 on a rung-skipping pair is evidence against the ladder or, far more often, against a single concept's type assignment. Read a hit as a prompt to revisit T(c) before the ladder.
